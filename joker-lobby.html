Challenge a lobby Joker

A lobby Joker sticks only if all six are true:
  1. You and the maker had a direct connection on SuiteFace (a Play, a Pair or a Play Hand).
  2. The maker signed the first-hand attestation.
  3. You hadn't blocked or reported the maker before the Joker.
  4. It isn't payback for your own Joker or report on them in the 14 days before.
  5. You two never both called a date a Win.
  6. The maker's account is open and they aren't currently a Joker.

If any one isn't true, or your situation isn't listed, the challenge is accepted and the Joker comes off.
