Six moves from stranger to pair

Every card in your deck passed your dealbreakers and fits what you're looking for. You decide who stays in your hand.

  1. 1Find your suit

    A two-minute quiz about how you show up in love. It decides which suit you play.

  2. 2Set your Face

    Your photo and answers. Each one stays face up or face down, your call.

  3. 3Call trumps

    Rank the four suits by who you click with. Your favorite leads your deck.

  4. 4Get your hand

    Thirteen people in each suit, Ace to 2. Only ID-verified members can be an Ace, King, Queen or Jack.

  5. 5Burn or play

    Burn a card and it's gone for good. Play a card to say you're interested.

  6. 6Make a pair

    When you both play each other's card, the Side Table opens and you can talk.
