3.3 House safety rules for every date. We strongly urge every member to:

  1. Meet in a busy public place for at least the first several dates: a restaurant, coffee shop or event. Never meet at a home, hotel room, car or any private or isolated place for a first date.
  2. Tell a friend or family member who you're meeting, where, and when you expect to be home. Share your live location with them if you can.
  3. Get yourself there and back. Drive yourself or use your own rideshare. Don't accept a ride from your date, and don't let them pick you up at home.
  4. Keep personal details private until you trust someone: your home address, workplace, schedule, financial information and your children's details. SuiteFace keeps phone numbers hidden until both players call a Win.
  5. Watch your drink. Know your limits with alcohol, never leave a drink unattended, and never accept a drink you didn't see poured.
  6. Never send money, gift cards, crypto or financial information to someone you haven't known for a long time in person, no matter the story.
  7. Trust your gut. Leave whenever you want. You never owe anyone your time, a second date, or physical contact. Consent can be withdrawn at any moment.
  8. Video chat first if you're unsure the person is who they say.
  9. Report anyone who pressures, threatens, harasses or scares you. Use Block or Report in the app.

6.2 Vote Jokers. After a confirmed Play Hand on one of the first three dates two members have together, a member who calls a Loss may make a Joker within 12 hours. From the fourth date on, a Loss can only burn the card, and the only way to make a Joker about that member is the Joker Maker Lobby. When a member receives 10 Jokers from 10 different members within 180 days, their card shows JOKER for 45 days. During the first 15 days (probation) the card stays visible and can't be paused or hidden. After probation, the member may clear it for $2,100.00 or wait out the 45 days.
