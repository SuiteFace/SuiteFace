Q: Why don't I have 52 cards yet?
A: Your deck only holds people who pass your gates (distance, ages, who you want to see, dealbreakers) and who want to see you too. Invite friends to fill your table, or widen your distance or ages.

A: Each card gets a 100-point fit score for you: values, your table, shared interests, trumps, truth, closeness and age. Ace, King, Queen and Jack go only to ID-verified members. See House Fit Rules.
Q: How do I get the verified Seal?
A: Verify with a driver's license, state ID or passport plus a quick live selfie. That confirms you're 18+ and that your face matches your card.
Q: How do Jokers work?
A: After a Loss on one of your first three dates together, you can make a Joker within 12 hours of Play Hand. Ten Jokers within 180 days shows JOKER on a card for 45 days. Every Joker can be challenged for free. See Joker Rules.
Q: Someone made me feel unsafe.
A: If you're in danger, call 911 now. Then use Report a safety concern on their card. Safety reports go straight to SuiteFace staff and are never handled as a Joker.
Q: I can't use 'Use my location'.
A: Your browser has to allow location for suiteface.com, and it only works on the secure site. You can always type your ZIP code or city instead.
Q: How do I change or delete my account?
A: Settings lets you edit your Face, change your @username if it's free, download your data, or delete your account.

A: Email help@suiteface.com. SuiteFace doesn't take phone calls; every request is answered by email so there's a written record.

# House Fit Rules

**How SuiteFace ranks your 52** · Algorithm best52-v2 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Your deck isn't a popularity contest and rank is never for sale. Every card is scored for **you**, from 0 to 100, with the same written rules for everyone. The rules lean on what relationship research has found, and on one house belief: **the more truthfully you fill in your Face, the better your deck gets.**

## Step 1: The house gates

Before anyone is scored, both people must pass every gate. Miss one and the card simply isn't dealt.

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| Seat | Paid, active member with a verified email and phone |
| Distance | Inside your chosen radius, measured ZIP to ZIP |
| Age | Inside your age range |
| Both ways | You fit who **they** want to see, and they fit who you want to see |
| Dealbreakers | Any answer you marked as a dealbreaker must match |
| History | Never burned, blocked or reported by either of you |

## Step 2: The fit score (100 points)

| Part | Points | IF … THEN |
|---|---|---|
| **Values** | 30 | **Kids plans (10):** both want kids or both don't → 10 · one is open → 8 · one unsure → 5 · one wants and one doesn't → 0. **Faith (10):** same faith → 10 · you're open-minded or open to a non-religious partner → 8 · "equal time" → 7 · you want the same faith and they differ → 1. **Politics (5):** same → 5 · one moderate or not political → 3.5 · opposite → 0.5. **Lifestyle (5):** same drinking and smoking habits score highest; sober vs. drinks often, or never vs. smokes regularly, scores 0. "Prefer not to say" always scores the middle. |
| **Your table** | 20 | Same table (what you're looking for) → 20 · neighboring tables (for example Long-term and Marriage-minded) → 12 · two steps apart → 6 · far apart → 2 · Open/ENM with a monogamous table → 0 |
| **Shared interests** | 15 | Points for each interest you share, up to 5 |
| **Your trumps** | 10 | Their suit is your #1 trump → 10 · #2 → 7 · #3 → 4 · #4 → 2 |
| **Truth** | 5 | Their own words match the words their friends and family chose for them → up to 5 |
| **Closeness** | 10 | Closer scores higher, inside your radius |
| **Age fit** | 5 | Nearer the middle of your range scores higher |
| **House tiebreak** | 5 | A fixed shuffle so equal scores don't always land the same way |

## Step 3: Rank

Everyone who passed the gates is lined up by suit, highest fit first. **Ace, King, Queen and Jack are face cards and go only to ID-verified members.** The next nine best fits in each suit become 10 down to 2. Four suits × 13 = your 52.

## Why these rules

- **Shared values and life plans carry the most weight.** Couples tend to match on religion and politics, and a mismatch on whether to have children is one of the plainest reasons relationships end, so Values is the biggest part of the score and can be made a hard dealbreaker.
- **Similarity sparks first attraction.** A large meta-analysis found that actual similarity (attitudes, interests, personality) strongly predicts attraction when people don't know each other well yet. That's exactly the moment a deck is dealt. (Montoya, Horton & Kirchner, 2008)
- **Who someone is matters, a little.** Partners who are kind, dependable, steady and outgoing tend to have happier relationships. Your suit and your trump order let you choose the style you click with. (Malouff et al., 2010)
- **No formula predicts love.** The largest study of its kind, 43 datasets and more than 11,000 couples, found that what happens *inside* a relationship (commitment, appreciation, how conflict goes) predicts satisfaction far better than anything known beforehand. (Joel et al., 2020) That's why SuiteFace deals you a deck instead of "the one," and why **Play Hand** results matter more than any score.
- **Truth is the house edge.** Scores are only as good as the answers. Members whose own words line up with what their people say about them earn the Truth points, and verified members are the only ones who can be face cards.
