A dating app for people who want something real

If you're done with games, start with a deck built for serious relationships.

Your goal, printed on your card

Every SuiteFace card sits at a table: Marriage-minded, Long-term, Dating to see, Friends first, Casual, or Open. The table's felt color shows on your card, so intentions are clear before the first message.

Fewer, better matches

Instead of thousands of profiles, you get best fits, 13 per suit, ranked Ace to 2 on values (kids, faith, politics, lifestyle), your table, shared interests, how you love, honesty, closeness and age.

Accountability built in

  • Face cards (A, K, Q, J) are ID-verified.
  • People who behave badly on early dates can earn a Joker from the people who met them.
  • A date only counts as a Win when both of you say so.

Fair price, with a guarantee

Free to join. $21 Buy-In, then $2.10 a month. If we can't deal you 12 cards within 10 days, ask for your $21 back.

How do I find people who want marriage?

Pick the Marriage-minded table. Your card wears its felt, and your deck favors people at the same table. You can also make "looking for" a dealbreaker.

Why does a dating app cost money?

SuiteFace charges a small $21 Buy-In and $2.10 a month. It keeps the table full of people who showed up on purpose, and funds ID verification and safety.
