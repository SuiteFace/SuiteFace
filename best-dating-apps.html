How to choose the best dating app for you

There's no single best dating app for everyone. The best dating app is the one built for what you actually want. Use this checklist to compare any app, including SuiteFace.

1. Does it ask what you're looking for?

Marriage, long-term, casual, friends first: an app should make intentions visible. On SuiteFace your table (what you're looking for) is printed on your card, so nobody has to guess.

2. Can you set real dealbreakers?

Faith, kids, distance, age, smoking, politics. Good apps let you make the important ones dealbreakers, not just preferences. SuiteFace's Rooms go further: play a deck of only members in the Christian Room, the Sober Room, the Parents Room and more.

3. Is identity verified?

Look for government-ID verification and live photos. On SuiteFace, only ID-verified members can be dealt as face cards (A, K, Q, J), and portraits taken in-app get a 📷 Live Photo badge.

4. How does it handle bad behavior?

Blocking and reporting should be easy, and repeat bad actors should be visible. SuiteFace's Joker system lets people who actually met someone flag bad behavior from early dates, with a free challenge for every Joker.

5. Is it endless, or finite?

Endless feeds keep you scrolling. A finite deck of best fits helps you focus on real people and real dates.

6. What does it cost, really?

Check the up-front price, the renewal, and how to cancel. SuiteFace is free to join, $21 a year for your seat, then $2.10 a deck, with a 12-Card Deck Guarantee. Launch offer: through December 31, 2026, the $21 Buy-In is waived and you join for $2.10 a month.

7. Does it help you meet safely?

The app should keep your number private until you're ready, and push safe habits: public places, your own ride, telling a friend. See our Safety Guide.

Questions

What is the best dating app for serious relationships?

Look for an app that asks what you're looking for up front, lets you set real dealbreakers, verifies identity, and limits endless swiping. SuiteFace puts your relationship goal on your card and deals a finite deck of best fits.

Are paid dating apps better than free ones?

Paying doesn't guarantee better matches, but a small fee tends to filter out people who aren't serious. Check exactly what you get, how renewal works, and how to cancel before you pay.

How can I tell if a dating profile is fake?

Be wary of profiles that won't video chat, move fast to another app, ask for money, or have stories that don't add up. Apps with ID verification and live photos make fakes harder.
