The dating app that deals you a real deck

Most dating apps hand you an endless feed. SuiteFace hands you a deck. Fifty-two people near you who fit what you're looking for, ranked from Ace to 2, so you spend your time on real possibilities instead of scrolling.

How SuiteFace works

  1. Find your suit. A two-minute quiz shows how you show up in love: ♥ Heart, ♦ Diamond, ♣ Club or ♠ Spade.
  2. Build your Face. Your photo (take it live for the 📷 Live Photo badge), a few lines about you, and what you're looking for.
  3. Pick your table. Marriage-minded, long-term, dating to see, friends first, casual, or open. Your card wears that table's felt so everyone knows what you're here for.
  4. Get your 52. We deal the best fits within your distance and ages who pass your dealbreakers, and who'd want to see you too.
  5. Burn, Play or Pair. Burn a card and it's gone for good. Play it to say you're interested. When you both play each other, it's a Pair and the Side Table opens so you can talk.
  6. Play the hand. Meet somewhere public, check in together, and each call the date: Win, Push or Loss.

Built for real dates

  • Verified face cards. Only ID-verified members can be dealt as Ace, King, Queen or Jack.
  • Jokers. Members who behave badly on early dates can be flagged by the people who met them. Ten flags shows JOKER on their card. Every Joker can be challenged.
  • Safety first. Your phone number stays hidden until you both call a Win. Meet in public, arrange your own ride, and call 911 in an emergency. Read the Safety Guide.

What it costs

Joining is free. The $21 Buy-In includes your first 30 days, then $2.10 a month. Cancel anytime. With the 12-Card Deck Guarantee, if we can't deal you at least 12 cards within 10 days, you can ask for your $21 back or stay while your table fills.

Questions

Is SuiteFace free?

Joining, building your profile and taking the suit quiz are free. The $21 Buy-In unlocks your full deck, then membership is $2.10 a month. If we can't deal you at least 12 cards within 10 days, you can ask for your $21 back.

How is SuiteFace different from swipe apps?

There's no endless scroll. You get a finite deck of 52 people who fit what you asked for and who'd want to see you too, 13 per suit, ranked Ace to 2. You Burn, Play, or make a Pair.

Are profiles verified?

Ace, King, Queen and Jack cards go only to members who verify with a government ID and a live selfie. Every member must confirm a phone and an email.
