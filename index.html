How it plays

Six moves from stranger to Pair

Most apps hand you an endless scroll. SuiteFace hands you a real deck: 52 people, no filler, no Jokers. Every card is someone who fits what you asked for.

  1. 1Find your suitA two-minute style quiz. Your suit is how you show up in love.
  2. 2Set your FacePhoto, Calling Card, a few lines about you. Shown only to your deck.
  3. 3Call trumpsRank the four suits by who you click with. Your trump leads your Hand.
  4. 4Get your 52The best fits near you who pass your gates: 13 of each suit, Ace to 2.
  5. 5Burn or PlayBurn a card and it's gone for good. Play a card to say you're interested.
  6. 6Make a PairWhen you both play each other's card, the Side Table opens and you can talk.
